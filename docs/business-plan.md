# "Dubai Scents" (working name): business, compliance, website & AI plan

## Context
Two 22-year-old co-founders in Chennai, both part-time with day jobs, want to build a brand that brings Dubai perfume oils to India. One founder holds a UAE residence visa and has parents and a brother in Dubai. The business has two lines:
- **Own-brand D2C line**, the flagship, sold to consumers:
  - Launch: alcohol-free attar roll-ons plus an "oil library" of small-size scent oils.
  - Phase 2: perfume sprays.
- **Generic B2B perfume oils**: sold by the kilo to Indian D2C perfume and attar brands and to attar shops.

The website must work as both store and brand flagship (Apple-style storytelling), and AI agents should do as much of the research, sourcing and lead-gen as possible. The starting point was an idea only. This plan is the outcome of an 8-round grilling session; every decision below was confirmed by the user. Year-1 phase-1 budget: **under ₹5L**.

## Positioning
**Alcohol-free, concentrated, crafted from Dubai oils.**
- **Story:** honest, with no invented heritage. Built from Dubai's perfumery tradition, the family's Dubai roots and the craft of the formulas. The lab is never named.
- **Longevity claims:** only after our own wear tests in Chennai humidity, as ASCI requires.
- **Claims we never make:** no "Halal" claims (UP 2023 risk); no "inspired by <designer>" names. Scents are described by profile instead.
- **Label origin wording:** "Made in India; fragrance oil imported from UAE". A premium "Bottled in Dubai" line may come later.
- **Name:** a new trademarkable name replaces "Dubai Scents". "Dubai" moves to the tagline.

## Business model & supply chain (settled)
- **Sourcing:** buy from **actual UAE manufacturers**. Traders are allowed for generic oils only, and only if they provide the manufacturer's authorisation, SDS and IFRA certificate.
  - Use **one manufacturer in year 1**, plus one backup.
  - Own-brand formulas are **exclusive to us for India**, with an escrow copy held.
- **Legal structure for the product:**
  - **CDSCO import registration** (Form COS-1 → COS-2) for the bulk oil.
  - A **loan licence** (COS-6 → COS-9, about ₹10k, about 45 days) with **one licensed contract filler**.
  - The filler fills the roll-ons and repacks bulk oil into our branded 500 g / 1 kg aluminium bottles.
  - The label names the filler as manufacturer, which keeps the Dubai source off the pack.
- **Filler shortlist:**
  - Chennai, outside any SEZ, first: **Argus Cosmetics** (Maraimalai Nagar) and **Clarion Cosmetics** (Irungattukottai).
  - Also talk to the MEPZ attar houses (Nayaab, Attar Mohamed Dawood). SEZ caveat: their sales into India count as imports.
  - Fallback: Kannauj or Mumbai.
  - Must-have: their licence covers **category 41, non-hydroalcoholic perfumes**, and they accept roughly 500-unit runs.
- **Own filling unit** only when there are about 5k units a month **and** one founder is full-time.
- **D2C range at launch** (alcohol-free, CDSCO category 41):
  - **3 hero own-brand roll-ons** (6/12 ml), 500 units each, priced ₹699–1,499.
  - **An "oil library"**: small sizes of the catalogue oils for anyone who wants them.
    - Start with 6 oils in 2 sizes (e.g. 3 ml and 12 ml), growing to 10 from revenue.
    - Each scent adds about $50 as a CDSCO variant.
    - Ask fillers for 100–200-unit plain-label runs.
- **B2B at launch:** bulk oils.
- **Phase 2:** alcohol-based perfume sprays, funded from the first B2B revenue. They bring:
  - a second CDSCO category (hydro-alcoholic perfumes),
  - a filler licensed for alcoholic perfumes and for handling perfumery alcohol (state permits to verify),
  - dangerous-goods shipping, surface only.
  - Alternatively, import finished sprays from the UAE at about 44% duty.
- **Stock:** consolidated monthly imports, with a 3–5-SKU buffer kept at the filler. Rare oils are ordered on demand at the buyer's cost.
- **Freight:** air first.
  - Require an SDS showing a **flash point above 60 °C** so shipments go as non-dangerous goods.
  - Move to sea LCL above about 100 kg.
  - Use our own Chennai customs broker (3 quotes).
- **B2B terms:** 500 g minimum per scent. 100% advance for each buyer's first 3 orders. A paid sample kit (about ₹999, 10 × 3 ml), credited against the first order.
- **B2C operations:**
  - Shiprocket, surface delivery. Prepaid, plus COD with a ₹49 fee.
  - No returns on opened perfume. A replacement for damage, with an unboxing video.
  - Ship from home in Chennai until about 300 orders a month.
- **Samples:**
  - Only declared trade samples (within ₹3L and 50 units a year).
  - Get CDSCO consumer-study permission from the Chennai port office.
  - No undeclared luggage stock for sale or for buyers.
- **Middleman bridge** (using a buyer's own CDSCO registration): dropped. The only exception is a paid sourcing-agent deal with a signed commission and non-circumvention agreement.

## Entity, people, money
- **Entity:** LLP, with both founders as designated partners. **Check both employment contracts first** (moonlighting clauses).
- **LLP agreement:**
  - 50/50 split.
  - 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff.
  - All IP (brand, formulas, code) belongs to the LLP.
  - Joint sign-off on spends over ₹25k and on any debt.
  - Buy-out formula if a founder leaves.
  - Lawyer review about ₹5–10k.
- **Roles:**
  - **Founder:** sourcing, the manufacturer relationship, CDSCO and compliance, product.
  - **Co-founder:** B2B sales and interviews, Instagram, website operations, customer service.
  - **Family in Dubai:** informal on-ground help.
  - **Father (accountant):** books, GST and import accounting. An independent CA friend signs any audits.
- **Home address:** the LLP shares it with the mother's GST registration, which is filed properly; the user is asking her whether it can be cancelled. The LLP needs its own NOC, a separate signboard, separate storage and separate books.
- **Registrations:** Udyam on day 1 (free). Apply for DPIIT, with the innovation case built on the AI layer. Trademark filed at the ₹4,500 rate if DPIIT or MSME status qualifies.
- **Later schemes to check:**
  - **UYEGP:** age-eligible at 22. Check whether it accepts an LLP or trading business.
  - **TANSEED:** needs DPIIT; target the next call after first revenue.
- **Debt:** none before the go/no-go gate. After it passes, a Mudra Kishore loan of ₹2–3L for stock if needed.

## Compliance checklist (in order)
1. Employment-contract check.
2. LLP incorporation. Separately, mom's GST: optional cancellation or continued filing.
3. Udyam, PAN/TAN, GST (Tamil Nadu), IEC (renewed yearly), DPIIT application.
4. Trademark (Class 3) for the chosen name.
5. Apply to CDSCO's Chennai port office for consumer-study permission for samples.
6. **Go/no-go gate passes**, then file CDSCO COS-1 on SUGAM.
   - Needs the manufacturer's authorisation letter, authenticated by the Indian Consulate or an accepted equivalent.
   - Also: IS 4707-2 undertaking, SDS, and labels at least 6 months from expiry at import.
   - Filed ourselves, plus a ₹5–10k consultant review.
7. Loan licence with the filler, from the state licensing authority where the filler sits. File it in parallel with step 6.
8. Legal Metrology importer registration (Tamil Nadu).
9. Labels:
   - **Rule 34:** filler name and address, manufacturing licence number, batch number, "Marketed by", "Made in India; fragrance oil imported from UAE", MRP, net quantity, customer-care contact.
   - **E-commerce:** the site needs a country-of-origin filter (Legal Metrology, from 1 Jul 2026).

## Website
- **Stack:** Next.js on **Vercel Hobby (free)**, **Supabase free** (Postgres and auth), **Razorpay**, and Shiprocket integration.
- **Upgrade only when needed.**
  - **Vercel:** Hobby's terms allow personal, non-commercial use only. Develop on it, but **move to Pro (or another host) before the site takes commercial traffic** (B2C checkout or paid sample kits).
  - **Supabase free:** pauses after 7 days idle, 500 MB, no automatic backups. The daily agents keep it active, and we run a weekly `pg_dump` backup.
- **Portability rules:**
  - Standard Postgres only.
  - No Vercel-only services (Edge Config, KV).
  - Business logic in SQL or plain API routes, so it can move to AWS, Cloudflare or a VPS later.
- **v1 (pre-approval):**
  - Cinematic brand-story pages using the real Dubai footage.
  - Product pages with a waitlist.
  - B2B trade-account application, sample-kit request or purchase, and quote request (RFQ) forms.
  - A journal for search traffic.
  - A WhatsApp button.
  - A private admin page for reviewing agent output.
  - English only, structured so Tamil and Hindi can be added.
- **v2 (on CDSCO approval):**
  - B2C checkout (prepaid plus COD fee).
  - GST invoices.
  - Country-of-origin filter.
  - Trade price lists behind login.
- **Imagery:** real product photos (a DIY macro kit now, a pro shoot later). AI is used only for backgrounds and scenes, never for the product itself.
- **Design system:** AI concepts in Figma, then the freelancer's final logo and label set the tokens.
