# Consumer survey — alcohol-free perfume oils (v1, 2026-10-20)

**Purpose:** test our B2C assumptions with real numbers before we build marketing on them — especially *"alcohol-free appeals to many Indian buyers, including Gen-Z"* (currently an **unvalidated hypothesis**), price band ₹699–1,499, and what "long-lasting" means to people.

**Audience & sample:** Indian fragrance buyers 18–45; aim for **150+ responses** (target ≥ 50 aged 18–25). Distribute via Instagram stories, college/alumni WhatsApp groups (with permission, not broadcast spam), r/IndianFragrances (follow sub rules), and friends-of-friends. Note the channel for each response — online samples skew young and fragrance-enthusiast; don't over-generalise.

**Tool:** Google Forms (free). ~4 minutes, 12 questions. No personal data required; the final email question is optional and opt-in (DPDP: state why we collect it and that they can ask us to delete it).

**Design rules followed:** neutral wording, balanced scales, "Other/Prefer not to say" options, no double-barrelled questions, randomise option order where possible, no mention of our brand until the last question (avoids bias).

---

**Intro text:**
> "We're two founders in Chennai researching how people in India choose and use fragrance. This takes about 4 minutes and is anonymous. Thank you!"

1. **How old are you?** 18–21 · 22–25 · 26–30 · 31–35 · 36–45 · 45+ · Prefer not to say
2. **Which city do you live in?** (short text)
3. **How often do you wear fragrance (any kind)?** Daily · A few times a week · Occasionally · Rarely · Never
4. **Which types have you used in the last 12 months?** (select all) Perfume spray (EDP/EDT) · Body mist / deo · Attar / perfume oil · Roll-on oil · Solid perfume · None · Other
5. **When you last bought a fragrance, what mattered most?** (pick up to 3, order randomised) How long it lasts · How strongly others notice it · The scent itself · Price · Brand · Alcohol-free · Recommendations/reviews · Packaging · Natural ingredients · Other
6. **How important is it to you that a fragrance is alcohol-free?** Not at all important · Slightly · Moderately · Very · Extremely important · Not sure
7. **If alcohol-free matters to you at all, why?** (select all; shown to everyone, with "It doesn't matter to me") Religious reasons · Skin sensitivity · Prefer natural-feeling products · Lasts longer on me · Personal/family preference · It doesn't matter to me · Other
8. **What's your experience with how long perfume oils/attars last compared with sprays?** Oils last longer · About the same · Sprays last longer · Haven't used oils · Not sure
9. **How noticeable do you want your fragrance to be?** Only people very close · People nearby · Noticeable across a room · Depends on the occasion
10. **For a 10–12 ml premium perfume oil roll-on that you love, what would you expect to pay?** Under ₹500 · ₹500–799 · ₹800–1,199 · ₹1,200–1,599 · ₹1,600–2,499 · ₹2,500+
11. **Where do you usually buy fragrance?** (select all) Brand websites · Amazon/Flipkart/Nykaa · Quick-commerce · Mall/brand store · Local attar/perfume shop · Abroad/travel retail · Other
12. **Does "fragrance oils from UAE manufacturers" make a product more appealing, less appealing, or make no difference to you?** More appealing · No difference · Less appealing · Not sure
13. *(Optional)* **Want early access when we launch? Leave your email.** "We'll only use this to tell you about our launch; reply anytime to be removed."

---

**Analysis plan (before seeing results):**
- Q6/Q7 by age band → does alcohol-free matter for 18–25 specifically? If < 30% of 18–25 rate it "very/extremely important", drop the Gen-Z angle from marketing.
- Q10 → validate ₹699–1,499 band (target: ≥ 50% choose ₹500–1,599).
- Q8/Q9 → shape honest longevity/projection messaging (oils sit closer to skin).
- Q12 → whether "UAE oils" is a selling point or neutral.
- Record findings + sample size in `.agents/product-marketing.md` → Learnings (status `watch` until confirmed by sales data).
