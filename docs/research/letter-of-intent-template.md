# Letter of Intent (non-binding) — template

> **How to use:** fill the bracketed fields after a buyer has evaluated samples and documents. It is deliberately **non-binding** — it records serious interest and indicative volumes so we can decide which oils to register with CDSCO. Print on letterhead once the LLP and brand name exist; until then use the founders' names. Optional: ask a lawyer to glance at it once (₹ minimal) before the first one goes out.

---

**LETTER OF INTENT**

Date: [DD Month YYYY]

**From (Buyer):** [Business name], [legal form if known], [address], GSTIN [if available]
Contact: [name, role, phone, email]

**To (Supplier):** [Brand / LLP name — once registered], Chennai, Tamil Nadu
Contact: [founder name, phone, email]

**1. Purpose.** This letter records the Buyer's intention to consider purchasing alcohol-free perfume oils from the Supplier, subject to the conditions below. It is not a purchase order.

**2. Products of interest.**

| Oil (supplier code / profile description) | Intended use (neat roll-on / diluted spray / other) | Indicative quantity per order | Indicative frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| [e.g. DS-07 — warm amber, saffron, dry woods] | [ ] | [ kg] | [monthly / quarterly] |
| | | | |

**3. Indicative commercial terms (for planning only).**
- Indicative price range discussed: [₹ ___ – ₹ ___ per kg], subject to final quotation.
- Minimum order per oil: [500 g]. First orders on advance payment.
- Delivery: [city], estimated from [month/year].

**4. Conditions.** Any future order is subject to:
- samples and production batches matching the approved sample;
- Safety Data Sheet (SDS), IFRA certificate of conformity and batch Certificate of Analysis (COA) being provided;
- the Supplier holding the required Indian registrations (CDSCO import registration and manufacturing/loan licence) before supply;
- agreement on final price and terms in a separate purchase order.

**5. Non-binding.** This letter is an expression of intent only. It does not create any obligation on either party to buy or sell, and either party may withdraw at any time without liability. Only the confidentiality clause below is binding.

**6. Confidentiality.** Both parties will keep confidential the commercial terms, samples, formulas and supplier information shared in connection with this letter.

**7. Validity.** This letter is valid for [6] months from the date above.
