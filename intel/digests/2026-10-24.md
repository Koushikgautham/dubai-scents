## 1. Regulation & trade
- **CDSCO is cracking down on imported cosmetics without COS-2.**
  - **What happened:** a 23 Jul 2026 circular reportedly asks zonal offices and state drug controllers to step up checks on imported cosmetics sold without import registration. A later public notice (8 Sep 2026) flagged two unregistered imported creams. Both are reported by secondary sources; we haven't seen the CDSCO originals. ([MRDx](https://mrdxregulatory.com/regulatory-updates/cdsco-vigilance-imported-cosmetics-without-import-registration-cos2-july-2026), [Regacats](https://blogs.regacats.in/cdsco-cosmetic-import-alert-unauthorised-cosmetics))
  - **Why it matters:** it confirms our rule. No imported oil is sold, and no sample is given as stock, until COS-2 is granted. Trade samples stay declared and not for sale.
  - **Action:** none; this supports the current plan.
- **Legal Metrology: country-of-origin filter on e-commerce.**
  - **What happened:** GSR 128(E), notified 13 Feb 2026 and in force since **1 Jul 2026**, makes e-commerce sites selling imported packaged goods offer a searchable country-of-origin filter. ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/02/21/legal-metrology-packaged-commodities-amendment-rules-2026-explained/))
  - **Why it matters:** this is already in the business plan. Still open: whether our India-filled roll-ons count as "imported products" on the site.
  - **Action:** keep on the Legal Metrology controller question list.
- **UP halal-mark order:** no 2026 ruling found; the latest reported Supreme Court listing was in early 2025. ([Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/supreme-court-notice-petitions-challenging-ban-halal-products-uttar-pradesh)) The status is unchanged for us: **no halal claims.**
- **ASCI:** the Aug 2026 summer-claims review found that 98% of the claims it examined needed correction. Unproven "instant results" claims were a common reason. It covered no fragrance ads, but it shows ASCI's current stance on unsupported performance claims. ([ASCI PDF](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Press-Release-The-Heat-Is-On.pdf)) This reinforces our rule: no longevity numbers without wear tests.
