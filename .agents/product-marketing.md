## Product Overview
- **Brand:** working name "Dubai Scents". It **will be replaced** by a trademarkable name, and "Dubai" moves to the tagline. Don't use "Dubai Scents" in final public copy.
- **What we sell:**
  1. **Own-brand D2C:** alcohol-free attar roll-ons (6/12 ml, 3 hero SKUs, ₹699–1,499), plus an **Oil Library** of small-size scent oils (3 ml / 12 ml).
  2. **B2B:** bulk perfume oils (500 g / 1 kg, branded aluminium bottles) for Indian perfume and attar brands and shops.
  3. **Phase 2:** perfume sprays (EDP).
- **Origin:** fragrance oils come from UAE manufacturers and are filled in India by a licensed contract manufacturer. The label must say: **"Made in India; fragrance oil imported from UAE."**
- **Stage:** pre-launch. Products need CDSCO approval before any sale (expected Apr–Jul 2027). Until then the site collects a waitlist, trade-account applications and sample-kit requests only.

## Target Audience
- **B2B (revenue engine):**
  1. Indian D2C perfume and attar brands that need oils to bottle.
  2. Traditional attar shops and perfume counters.
- **B2C (brand builder):** Indian buyers who want long-wearing, alcohol-free fragrance with Middle-Eastern character. That includes people who avoid alcohol for religious, cultural or skin reasons.
- **Assumption under test:** that Gen-Z is drawn to "alcohol-free". It's a hypothesis to validate in interviews and the survey, not a fact. Don't build copy on it until validated.

## Personas
- **D2C brand founder:** sources oils for their own label. Cares about consistency, minimum order quantities, documentation (SDS, IFRA), price per kg and exclusivity. **TBD from interviews.**
- **Attar shop owner:** buys by the kilo. Cares about price, the scent profile customers ask for, and reliable resupply. **TBD from interviews.**

## Competitive Landscape
- **Direct:** Ajmal (Indian retail via Shoppers Stop and Health & Glow), the Lattafa India site, Kannauj attar houses (e.g. white-label OEMs), Indian D2C attar brands.
- **Secondary:** grey-import resellers of Armaf, Rasasi, Al Haramain and Swiss Arabian (fakes are common).
- **Indirect:** alcohol-based Western EDPs.
- Use `competitor-profiling` to build out the profiles, with URLs.

## Differentiation
- Alcohol-free, concentrated oils from UAE manufacturers, with formulas **exclusive to us for India**.
- Full documentation (SDS, IFRA, batch records) for B2B buyers.
- An honest founder story: two Chennai co-founders with family roots in Dubai.
- **Longevity claims:** only after our own wear tests in Chennai humidity. "Lasts in the desert, so it lasts in India" is **not** a valid argument, because Dubai is dry and Chennai is humid.

## Brand Voice
- Quiet luxury: warm, precise, unhurried. Apple-like restraint, Dubai warmth.
- **Banned claims and phrasing:**
  - No "Halal" claims or marks (Uttar Pradesh 2023 order risk).
  - No "inspired by <designer brand>" or "<brand> type" names; describe scent profiles instead.
  - No invented heritage ("since 1978").
  - No "award-winning".
  - No longevity numbers (e.g. "24-hour") without test data (ASCI).
  - Never imply the product is made in UAE.
- Full voice guide comes with the `brand-voice` skill, after the name is chosen.
