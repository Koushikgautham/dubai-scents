# Labelling — what must be on the pack (researched 2026-10-18/20)

Sources: Cosmetics Rules 2020 (CDSCO PDF) — https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/resources/UploadCDSCOWeb/2022/cos_rules/Cosmetics%20Rules%202020.pdf ; Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011, Rule 6 (secondary sources) ; LM amendment 2026 (SCC Online).

## A. Cosmetics Rules 2020 — Rule 34 (labelling of cosmetics)
For products **filled in India by a contract manufacturer** (our model):
- **Name of the cosmetic.**
- **Name and complete address of the manufacturer** — where made at someone else's factory (contract/loan licence), the **actual manufacturer's** name and address must appear (Rule 34(1)(b)). Containers **≤ 60 ml (liquid)** may show name, principal place of manufacture and **PIN code** instead of the full address.
- **Manufacturing licence number** — "M.L. No. …" (the filler's / loan licence).
- **Batch number** — *exempt for containers ≤ 25 ml* (verify exact wording for perfume before relying on it).
- **Net content** — Cosmetics Rules exempt perfume ≤ 60 ml from net content, **but Legal Metrology still applies** (see B).
- **Date of manufacture and expiry / "use before"** — *verify* exact format requirement in Rule 34 for perfumes.
- **Ingredients list** — *verify* requirement and size thresholds for perfumes/attars in Rule 34.
- **Warnings / directions for use** where applicable (e.g. "For external use only", "Patch test before use", "Keep away from flame" if flammable).
- **"Marketed by"** line for the brand owner is permitted alongside the manufacturer: "Mfd. by <filler>, <address>, M.L. No. <…>; Marketed by <brand LLP>, <address>".
- **Coded manufacturer names are only allowed for exports** (Rule 34(10)) — there is **no lawful way to hide the actual manufacturer on Indian packs**.

For **finished products imported already packed** (phase 2 option): also **RC No. (CDSCO registration certificate number) and the RC holder/importer name and address** (Rule 34(5)/(9)); stickering allowed at a bonded warehouse.

## B. Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules — Rule 6 (retail packages)
- **Name and address of the manufacturer / packer / importer** (for imported packages: importer's name and address).
- **Country of origin** — mandatory for imported packages. For goods *filled in India from imported bulk*, origin wording is legally unclear → we use **"Made in India; fragrance oil imported from UAE"** (*verify* with LM controller).
- **Common or generic name** of the commodity (e.g. "Perfume oil" / "Attar").
- **Net quantity** in standard units (ml or g) — applies even to small packs unless an LM exemption applies (*verify* small-pack exemptions).
- **Month and year of manufacture.**
- **MRP (inclusive of all taxes)** — "MRP ₹___ (incl. of all taxes)".
- **Unit sale price** where required (*verify* applicability to small cosmetic packs).
- **Consumer care details** — phone number and/or email (and address) for complaints.
- **Importer registration** with the State Controller under Rule 27 (for us as importer of bulk; see registrations).
- **E-commerce:** from **1 Jul 2026**, e-commerce platforms/sites must offer a **country-of-origin filter** and display the mandatory declarations on the product page.

## C. Our standard label blocks (templates)

**Roll-on / vial (≤ 60 ml, India-filled):**
```
<Product name> — Perfume Oil (alcohol-free)
Net: 6 ml
Mfd. by: <Filler name>, <city> <PIN> · M.L. No. <…>
Marketed by: <Brand LLP>, Chennai <PIN>
Made in India; fragrance oil imported from UAE
Batch: <…> (if > 25 ml or as required) · Mfg: MM/YYYY · Use before: MM/YYYY
MRP ₹___ (incl. of all taxes)
Customer care: <phone> · <email>
For external use only. Patch test before use. Keep away from eyes and flame.
```

**Bulk B2B bottle (500 g / 1 kg):** same blocks with full manufacturer address, batch number, net weight, and — for B2B trade (not retail) — note that some LM retail-package rules may not apply to institutional/industrial sales (*verify*); still include SDS reference and batch.

## D. Common label mistakes to catch
- "Made in Dubai / UAE" on an India-filled product.
- Missing actual manufacturer or M.L. No.
- MRP without "incl. of all taxes".
- No consumer-care contact.
- Halal logos/marks; designer names; untested longevity numbers on pack (see claims.md).
- "100% pure / natural oud" without composition evidence.
