# Import & shipping (researched 2026-10-18/20 — confirm duty with the customs broker on ICEGATE before each first import)

## HS codes and duty (normal rates; verify)
| HS | Goods | BCD | Approx. landed duty (BCD + 10% SWS on BCD + 18% IGST) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3302.90 | Perfumery compounds / perfume oils (our bulk import) | ~10% (verify) | **~31%** |
| 3303.00 40/50 | Perfumes & toilet waters (finished; 40 = no spirit, 50 = spirit) | 20% | **~44%** |
| 3301 | Essential oils | 20% (CEPA phasing) | ~44% |

- **Classification risk (verify in writing with the customs broker before pricing):** ready-to-fill perfume oil in a carrier may be classified as **3303 00 40 "attars of all kinds in fixed oil bases" (~44%)** instead of 3302.90 compound (~31%) — the difference (~11 points of BCD+SWS) is a real, non-recoverable cost. Get a written classification opinion (or an advance ruling) per product type.
- **India–UAE CEPA:** the 2022 tariff tables show **no concession for 3302.90 or 3303**; tables were replaced by Notification 09/2026-Customs (31 Mar 2026) — **not verified**, ask the broker. CEPA origin rule for Ch. 33: CTSH + 40% value added; simple mixing/dilution doesn't confer origin.
- GST (IGST) paid at import is creditable against output GST — keep the bill of entry (father/CA).

## Dangerous goods
- Perfumery products with **flash point ≤ 60 °C** are typically **UN1266, Class 3** for air: DG declaration, UN-spec packing, quantity limits, surcharges (~+US$100–250/shipment). Many Indian domestic air couriers decline perfume → plan **surface** for B2C.
- Most attar/perfume **oils** have flash points above 60 °C and may ship non-DG — **require an SDS showing flash point** per oil before booking.

## Samples
- **Personal baggage:** the free allowance (₹75,000 under Baggage Rules 2026) covers **personal use only** — not trade samples or stock.
- **Commercial samples:** customs exemption for bona fide samples (Notif. 154/94-Cus as amended by 29/2024): up to **₹3,00,000 and 50 units per year** — declare them.
- **CDSCO:** unregistered cosmetics can enter for **consumer studies/R&D only with CDSCO permission** (port office NOC); no sale or free distribution as stock.
- Enforcement: **Glamstone Cosmetics v. UoI** (Bombay HC, WP 957/2026, 9 Mar 2026) — unregistered cosmetics from Dubai treated as prohibited goods; seizure upheld, re-export refused.

## Shelf life & documents per shipment
- ≥ 6 months remaining shelf life at import (CDSCO).
- Commercial invoice, packing list, AWB/BL, certificate of origin, **SDS** (with flash point and transport section), **COA** per batch, IFRA certificate, CDSCO COS-2 copy, IEC, importer LM registration.

## Logistics rules of thumb
- Air: ~US$1–2.5/kg non-DG with minimum charges (~US$80–150); CHA (customs broker) ~₹3–10k per AWB; 1–2 days transit + 2–5 days clearance.
- Sea LCL from Jebel Ali: worth it from ~100 kg; ~2–3 weeks door to door; fixed destination charges dominate small lots.
- **Never**: under-declare value, mis-declare HS, split into "gifts", or hand-carry commercial stock.
