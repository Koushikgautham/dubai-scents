## CDSCO import registration — Form COS-1 → Certificate COS-2
- **Law:** Cosmetics Rules 2020, Rules 12–13. "No cosmetic shall be imported unless registered." Bulk **ready-to-fill** finished formulation is covered — **Rule 12(4) proviso:** the application must include the **Indian manufacturing (or loan) licence** for the finished product and the **brand owner's details**. No raw-material exemption found.
- **Portal:** CDSCO **SUGAM** (online).
- **Applicant:** manufacturer, its authorised agent, Indian subsidiary, or the importer (us) with the **manufacturer's authorisation, authenticated** (apostille / Indian embassy-consulate / magistrate as accepted). The UAE is not (to our knowledge — verify) a Hague Apostille party, so the usual route is **UAE notary → UAE MOFA attestation → Indian Consulate Dubai attestation**; budget time for it during the December trip.
- **Certificate content:** product/brand, variants, pack sizes, **actual manufacturer and premises** — so every oil/variant we import must be listed.
- **Fees (verify on SUGAM):** researched as **US$1,000 per product category + US$500 per manufacturing site + US$50 per variant** (one secondary source said US$2,000/category — confirm).
- **Timeline:** CDSCO target ~90 days; rules allow up to 6 months. Valid indefinitely with retention fee every 5 years.
- **Other requirements:** IS 4707 (Part 2) undertaking (fragrance contains no banned ingredients); **≥ 6 months remaining shelf life at import**; free-sale/manufacturing evidence and product information per CDSCO checklist.
- **Second-importer route:** COS-4 → COS-4A (import number, 3 years) lets another importer bring in products already registered under someone else's COS-2 (no manufacturer authorisation required by the rule text). Relevant both as an option and as a competitive risk.
- **R&D / consumer-study exemption:** imports for R&D, packaging trials, **consumer studies**, shelf-life and transport studies are exempt from registration **with CDSCO permission** (port office NOC); none may be sold.

## Loan licence (contract manufacturing) — Form COS-6 → Licence COS-9
- **Authority:** State Licensing Authority (Drugs Control) where the **filler's site** is (Tamil Nadu Drugs Control for Chennai fillers).
- **Fee:** ₹10,000 per category for up to 10 items + ₹500 per extra item (Third Schedule).
- **Timeline:** grant within ~45 days of application, then site inspection within ~30 days. Permanent with retention fee every 5 years.
- **Category for our oils:** **category 41 — non-hydroalcoholic perfumes** (the host filler's licence must cover it). Alcohol sprays (phase 2) need the hydro-alcoholic perfume category.
- **Documents:** COS-7 GMP self-declaration, constitution papers (LLP deed), host site's licence and consent, product list. Whether we need our own office/technical person in TN — **verify** with TN Drugs Control.
- **Why we need it:** repacking/relabelling is "manufacture" under Drugs & Cosmetics Act s.3(f).

## Own manufacturing licence (later) — COS-5 → COS-8
Whole-time technical staff (e.g. D.Pharm, 12th with Chemistry, or cosmetic-technology degree), premises meeting Seventh Schedule GMP (attars: authority decides adequacy), testing arrangements. Trigger in plan: ~5k units/month and a full-time founder.

## Legal Metrology — importer registration
Rule 27, Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules — register as importer with the **State Controller** (Tamil Nadu); fee nominal (Delhi ₹500 as reference). Packer registration may also apply to the filler.

## Business registrations
- **IEC** (DGFT, online, ~₹500) — update annually.
- **GST** — registration in TN; add the **filler's premises as an additional place of business** if our stock is stored there; the home address can host the LLP's GSTIN alongside another registration with NOC, separate signboard, storage and books.
- **Udyam** (MSME) — free; traders allowed for priority lending.
- **DPIIT Startup India** — free; innovation case needed (AI layer); unlocks startup trademark fee and TANSEED eligibility.
- **Trademark** Class 3 — ₹4,500 (startup/MSME/individual) vs ₹9,000 (others) per class; search https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/eregister/ first. Consider Class 35 (retail) later.
