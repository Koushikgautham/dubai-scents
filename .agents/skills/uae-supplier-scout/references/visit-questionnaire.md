# Visit questionnaires

Tailor to the company: drop what's already answered (check its notes file), put its open questions and red flags first. Bring a printed copy and a notebook; ask to see things, not just hear about them.

## A. UAE manufacturer visit
**Who you are (opening, 30 seconds):** "We're launching an alcohol-free perfume-oil brand in India — own-brand roll-ons and bulk oils for Indian perfume brands. We're choosing one manufacturing partner this month."

**1. Are you the manufacturer?**
- Can we see the production area, compounding and filling? Who are your perfumers?
- Which trade licence/free-zone licence do you hold? Any certifications (e.g. ISO 22716)?

**2. Documents (deal-breakers)**
- SDS, IFRA certificate (which amendment?), COA per batch, allergen declaration — can you send them for the oils we sample?
- We sell the oil **neat** in roll-ons: does the IFRA certificate allow use at ~100% in Category 4 (leave-on skin fragrance)? A certificate that only allows a low % covers spray-makers, not us.
- Flash point of these oils? Can you formulate alcohol-free oils above 60 °C?

**3. Indian import registration**
- Will you sign a manufacturer authorisation letter naming our company for CDSCO registration and get it authenticated (Indian consulate / accepted route)? Have you done this for any Indian importer before?
- Can you provide the formula/ingredient information CDSCO needs (and keep the rest confidential)?
- Shelf life of your oils? (We need ample remaining shelf life at import.)

**4. Our own scents**
- Can you create or adapt original formulas for us? Exclusivity for India — for how long, on what volume?
- Formula escrow: will you deposit the formula with a neutral party / give us a sealed copy so we can switch makers if needed?
- Batch consistency: retained samples per batch? What tolerance on COA values?

**5. Commercial**
- Price per kg at 1 / 5 / 10 / 25 / 50 kg; MOQ per oil; sample sizes and cost; lead time; payment terms; Incoterms (EXW/FOB/CIF to Chennai); packaging for export (UN-spec if DG).
- Private label support? Export experience to India; references we can call?

**6. Close**
- Next steps, who's our contact, by when we'll get samples + documents.

**Watch for:** designer-copy offers, "Made in Dubai" label offers, vague answers on documents or authorisation letter. Be polite; note it.

## B. Indian contract filler visit (e.g. Chennai)
- Licence number and validity; does it cover **category 41 non-hydroalcoholic perfumes**? Can we see the licence?
- Will you host our **loan licence** (COS-6 → COS-9)? Have you done loan licences before? What do you need from us?
- Minimum run for 6/12 ml roll-ons; for 3 ml oil-library vials; bulk repack into 500 g / 1 kg aluminium bottles?
- Do you source bottles/rollers/caps or do we supply? Labelling and batch coding?
- QC: incoming oil check, retained samples, batch records.
- Pricing per unit (filling, labour, components), lead time, storage of our buffer stock.
- Are you inside an SEZ? (If yes, sales into India are treated as imports — discuss.)

## On-site inspection table (use for both manufacturers and fillers)

| Look at | Good sign | Red flag |
|---|---|---|
| Licence on the wall / in hand | Licence copy shown; categories and validity match what we need | "Licence is with the owner", or it covers a different product type |
| Production floor | Separate compounding/filling area, clean, labelled tanks and lines | Only a shop/warehouse; "factory is elsewhere" with no visit offered |
| Filling line for small bottles | Semi-auto roll-on/vial filler, ball/cap pressing, sensible changeover cleaning | Hand-filling everything with no records; shared lines with aerosols/talc without separation |
| Raw-material / oil storage | Cool, dark, labelled drums with batch numbers | Unlabelled drums, heat, sunlight |
| QC corner | Retained samples by batch, balance, basic tests, batch records | No retained samples, "we check by smell" only |
| Documents | SDS/IFRA/COA in a file, sent on request | "We'll send later" for everything |
| Labels & batch coding | Batch/expiry coder, label applicator, sample finished packs | No coding capability |
| Safety | Fire extinguishers, ventilation (oils/alcohol are flammable) | None visible |
| People | A named technical person / perfumer who answers technical questions | Only sales staff; technical questions deflected |
| Payments | Invoices on letterhead to a company bank account | Cash-only, personal accounts, pressure for big deposits |

## C. Customs broker call
- Licensed customs broker? Experience clearing CDSCO-registered cosmetics at Chennai air cargo? DG shipments?
- Per-shipment fees (itemised), typical clearance time, documents they need from us.
