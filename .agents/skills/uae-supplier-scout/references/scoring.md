# Scoring and red flags

Score 0–10. Start from the must-haves; a single **deal-breaker** caps the score at 3 regardless of other strengths.

## Manufacturer score (weights)
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Confirmed actual manufacturer (factory + licence) | 2 |
| Will sign CDSCO authorisation letter | 2 |
| SDS + IFRA + COA available per oil | 2 |
| Formula exclusivity for India + escrow | 1.5 |
| Alcohol-free oils, flash point > 60 °C available | 1 |
| Workable MOQ / samples / lead time | 1 |
| India export experience, references | 0.5 |

**Deal-breakers:** trader posing as manufacturer; refuses authorisation letter; no SDS/IFRA at all; insists on designer-copy names on our labels; offers to ship via luggage or under-declare.

## Filler score (weights)
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Valid licence covering category 41 (checked on register) | 3 |
| Willing to host loan licence | 2 |
| Small runs (100–500 units) + roll-on & bulk repack capability | 2 |
| Non-SEZ, Chennai/TN location | 1.5 |
| GMP impression, documentation, price transparency | 1.5 |

**Deal-breakers:** expired/no licence; licence doesn't cover perfumes and they won't extend it; refuses loan licence.

## Red flags (note them in `red_flags`)
- "Same as <brand>", "<designer> type" naming.
- Prices far below market with no documents.
- Only a retail/souk address; no factory.
- "No need for paperwork, we ship to India all the time" / luggage shipping.
- Can't name the actual manufacturer of the oil they sell.
- "100% pure oud / Taif rose" at commodity prices.
- Pressure to pay large deposits before samples and documents.
- Website/listing with no physical address or recent activity.
