# Where to find leads

## D2C perfume / attar brands
- **Web search queries:** "attar brand India online", "alcohol-free perfume oil India brand", "roll-on attar D2C", "Indian perfume oil brand Shopify", "buy attar online <city>", "Kannauj attar brand online".
- **Store fingerprints:** many D2C brands run Shopify — search `"attar" site:myshopify.com`, or look for "Powered by Shopify" on brand sites.
- **Marketplaces:** brand pages on Amazon.in, Flipkart, Nykaa, Myntra, quick-commerce (Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart) category "attar"/"perfume oil" — note the brand, then find its own site/Instagram.
- **Instagram:** hashtags #attar #attarlover #perfumeoil #indianfragrance #alcoholfreeperfume #ittar; check business profiles (category "Health/beauty", "Brand"), link in bio, follower count, recency.
- **Community:** r/IndianFragrances brand mentions, fragrance YouTubers' "Indian brands" videos (note which brands they review).
- **Trade fairs:** Cosmoprof India (Mumbai, 10–12 Dec 2026) exhibitor list; Sugandh India (Indore); Agarbatti & Perfume Expo (Kolkata).
- **Startup databases:** news of seed-funded fragrance D2C brands (Inc42, YourStory, Entrackr) — funded brands buy more volume.

## Attar shops / perfume counters
- **Google Maps:** "attar shop" / "perfume shop" / "ittar" in Chennai neighbourhoods (Triplicane, T. Nagar, Parrys/George Town, Mylapore, Royapettah), then other cities. Use rating count and recent reviews as size signals.
- **Justdial / IndiaMART:** "attar dealers <city>", "perfume oil wholesalers <city>" (wholesalers can be buyers *and* competitors — note which).
- **In person:** best for Segment B — plan a walking route; the skill can produce a route list for a morning.
