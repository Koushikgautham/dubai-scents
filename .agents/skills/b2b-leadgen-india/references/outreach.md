# Outreach drafts

Purpose of a first message: **book a 20–30 minute discovery conversation**, not sell. Personalise with one specific, true observation about their business. Short (≤ 90 words), plain, no hype, no claims we don't make. Always end with the opt-out line. The founders send it.

## First message — D2C brand (email or Instagram DM)
> Hi <first name / team>, I'm <founder>, co-founder of a new Chennai fragrance company. I came across <brand> through <specific: your Kannauj rose roll-on / your Nykaa listing / a review by X> and liked <specific detail>.
> We're setting up to supply alcohol-free perfume oils from UAE manufacturers to Indian brands, and before we build anything we're talking to founders about how they source oils today — what works and what's painful. Would you be open to a 20-minute call this week or next? No pitch, just questions.
> If this isn't relevant, just say so and we won't follow up. Thanks!

## First message — attar shop (in person first; WhatsApp only if the number is published for business)
> Vanakkam / Salaam <name>, I'm <founder> from Chennai. We're starting a perfume-oil business and learning from experienced attar sellers how the trade works today. Could I visit for 15 minutes at a quiet time this week to ask a few questions? Happy to come whenever suits you. If you'd rather not, no problem at all.

## Follow-up (once, after 5–7 days, only if no reply)
> Hi <name>, just floating this up once in case it got buried — a quick 20-minute chat about how you source oils would really help us. If the timing's wrong or it's not relevant, no worries and I won't message again.

## After an interview (thank-you + January sample offer)
> Thanks for your time today, <name> — <one thing you learned>. As mentioned, we'll have samples from our UAE manufacturers in January; I'll reach out then to see if you'd like to evaluate a few.

## Don'ts
No "Made in Dubai", no "halal", no designer names, no longevity numbers, no prices before the December sourcing trip, no attachments/catalogues in first messages, no mass sends.
