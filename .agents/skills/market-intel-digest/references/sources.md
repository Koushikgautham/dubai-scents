# Where to look (weekly)

## Regulation & trade
- CBIC customs notifications (tariff + non-tariff), especially Chapter 33 and India–UAE CEPA tables: cbic.gov.in, taxinformation.cbic.gov.in; EEPC notification listings.
- CDSCO cosmetics notices, SUGAM updates, Cosmetics Rules amendments: cdsco.gov.in (Cosmetics section).
- Legal Metrology (Department of Consumer Affairs) amendments; Tamil Nadu Legal Metrology.
- DGFT trade notices (IEC, import policy).
- ASCI monthly complaint reports (personal care / fragrance), CCPA advisories.
- Court rulings: LiveLaw / Bar & Bench (trademark perfume cases, customs seizures of cosmetics).

## Competitors & market
- Indian D2C fragrance news: Inc42, YourStory, Entrackr, Economic Times Retail, Retail4Growth, BW Businessworld.
- Middle-Eastern brands in India: Ajmal, Lattafa (lattafaindia.com), Armaf, Rasasi, Al Haramain, Swiss Arabian, My Perfumes Select; store openings, marketplace launches.
- Quick-commerce and marketplace category moves (Nykaa, Blinkit, Zepto) for attars/perfume oils.

## Prices & supply
- AED→INR reference rate (RBI reference / a reputable FX site) with date.
- Air/sea freight notes Dubai/Sharjah → Chennai; DG rule changes (IATA).
- Gulf Business, Gulf News, The National, Khaleej Times: UAE perfume industry, oud/agarwood supply, Beautyworld Middle East.

## Trends & customers
- r/IndianFragrances, fragrance YouTube/Instagram creators (notable launches, alcohol-free discussion), Google Trends India for "attar", "perfume oil", "alcohol free perfume".

## Opportunities
- StartupTN (TANSEED calls), Startup India / DPIIT, ICDIC (UYEGP), MSME schemes; trade-fair dates (Cosmoprof India, Sugandh India, Beautyworld ME).
