# Nabeel Perfume Industries FZE
- **Type / location:** Manufacturer (own-brand group), Plot D4 10 & 11, SAIF Zone, Sharjah
- **Sources:** https://exhibitor.beautyworldme.com/exhibitor-search-en/exhibitor-search.detail.html/nabeel-perfume-industries-fze.html ; https://khaleejtimes.com/business/local/sensing-fragrant-success ; https://www.gdnonline.com/Details/34959 (checked 2026-10-20)

## What we know
- Beautyworld Middle East 2022 exhibitor listing: SAIF Zone address, www.nabeel.com, +971 56 409 9108, operations@nabeel.com. Product groups include Compounds and Fragrance Oil. Founded 1969 by Asghar Adam Ali.
- Four own brands (Nabeel, Chris Adams, The Spirit of Dubai, Nuvo). The range includes concentrated perfume oils. They claim to sell in 100+ countries.
- Press (~2014–2016): set up in SAIF Zone in 2002 with a semi-automatic plant, fully automatic plant from 2011, ~200,000 sq ft and 550+ staff. These are older figures and need re-checking.
- No private-label / OEM offer found on the listing or in search. A competitor's self-published "top 9" list (luxeolabs.com) claims Nabeel does "basic" private label. That source is not independent and is not relied on here.

## Open questions (ask on visit)
- Do you accept private-label perfume-oil orders from small brands? Minimum per oil?
- Is bulk oil (kg) available, or only filled bottles?
- SDS / IFRA (neat Cat 4 use) / COA per oil; flash points; authorisation letter for CDSCO; exclusivity + escrow.
- Any existing Indian importer of Nabeel / Chris Adams products?
