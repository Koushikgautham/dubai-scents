# CPL Aromas FZE (CPL Aromas Middle East)
- **Type / location:** Fragrance-compound manufacturer (UK-owned fragrance house), Jebel Ali Free Zone, Dubai
- **Sources:** https://cplaromas.com/cpl-aromas-middle-east ; https://www.happi.com/contents/view_breaking-news/0000-00-00/cpl-aromas-opens-site-in-dubai-96311/ ; https://gulfnews.com/business/retail/uks-cpl-aromas-opens-fragrance-factory-in-dubai-1.1522849 ; https://beautyworld-saudi-arabia.ae.messefrankfurt.com/ksa/en/exhibitor-search.detail.html/cpl-aromas-fze.html (checked 2026-10-20)

## What we know
- Trade press (Happi; Gulf News ~2015) reported that CPL opened a 10,000 m² compounding plant in JAFZA in May 2015, with a stated capacity of 200 t of fragrance concentrates a month and 75 staff. The Gulf News article said the plant *would* serve the GCC, India and North Africa. That was a plan stated at opening; it does not confirm current India sales.
- Company's own page (self-claim) calls Dubai its Middle East HQ and regional manufacturing centre, with 100+ staff. Its listed markets are the GCC, the Levant and NE Africa.
- A Beautyworld Saudi Arabia 2025 exhibitor listing still shows CPL Aromas FZE in JAFZA, per search result. We could not open the company page directly (certificate error).
- Business model: sells fragrance concentrates to brands and manufacturers. Concentrates are what perfume oils are made from, so CPL could supply our own-brand formulas at the formula level. A filler or blender would still be needed.

## Open questions (ask on visit)
- Minimum batch per fragrance (we need 2–5 kg per oil to start). Do you sell to start-ups?
- Do your IFRA certificates allow ~100% use in Category 4? Compounds are often certified only for a % in a spray. We sell neat oil.
- Flash points for oil-soluble compounds; can you formulate >60 °C?
- Exclusivity for India and formula ownership/escrow for commissioned scents.
- Authorisation letter for CDSCO registration. The importer must cite the *manufacturer*: is that CPL, or the blender/filler?

## Red flags
- None about legitimacy. Fit risk: MOQs and pricing are aimed at industrial customers. It may not private-label or fill.
