# Ahmed Al Maghribi Perfume Manufacturing LLC
- **Type / location:** Manufacturer (branded house with own factory), Jurf Industrial Area 3, Ajman
- **Sources:** https://beautyworld-saudi-arabia.ae.messefrankfurt.com/ksa/en/exhibitor-search.detail.html/ahmed-al-maghribi-perfume-manuf-llc.html ; https://www.perfumenetwork.in/collections/special/products/ahmed-al-maghribi-oud-roses ; https://gulfindustryonline.com/ArticleTA/430684 (all checked 2026-10-20)

## What we know
- Beautyworld Saudi Arabia exhibitor listing (2025 edition, per search result) gives the address as Al Bahia, Jurf Industrial Area 3, Ajman. Phone +971 4 285 5788 (from that listing).
- **Third-party evidence of a factory and of India imports:** an Indian online retailer (perfumenetwork.in) shows the product label: "Manufacturer: AHMED PERFUME MANUFACTURING LLC, PO BOX 3890, WAREHOUSE- FACTOR NO. 1, JURF INDUSTRIAL ZONE 3, AJMAN, UAE" and "Importer: Sharaya Cosmocare Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi". So their finished products already reach India through an Indian importer. This does *not* by itself tell us whether they would sign an authorisation letter for us, or whether they have done so before.
- Gulf Industry article (trade press, undated in our check) describes them as a large GCC manufacturer/wholesaler/retailer/exporter making oud, bakhoor and "oil-base" perfume blends as well as sprays.
- Store count varies by source (~130 to ~180 outlets); exports claimed to 160+ countries. Self-reported figures repeated by press.
- Catalogue names seen (e.g. "Oud & Roses") are original, not designer copies; full catalogue not reviewed.

## Open questions (ask on visit)
- Do you do private label / bulk perfume oil for other brands at all? Minimum per oil (we start ~15–20 kg total across several oils)?
- Who handled the CDSCO registration for Sharaya Cosmocare - did you sign a manufacturer authorisation letter? Would you sign one for us?
- SDS, IFRA certificate (allowing ~100% neat use, Cat 4) and COA per oil; flash points of the oil range (>60 °C?).
- Exclusivity for India and formula escrow for our own scents - possible, given they sell their own brand in India?

## Red flags
- None found. Risk: a large own-brand house may not want small private-label runs, and it may see our India brand as a competitor to its own.
