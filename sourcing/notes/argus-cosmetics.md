## Open questions (ask on visit)
1. Does licence C-1185 cover **category 41, non-hydroalcoholic perfumes**? Ask to see the licence and its product/category list. If not, will they apply to add it, and how long would that take?
2. Will they host our **loan licence** (COS-6 → COS-9)? Have they hosted one for another brand before? What do they need from us?
3. Do they do **third-party / private-label** work at all? For whom (names not needed, just the kind of client)?
4. Have they ever filled **neat (alcohol-free) oils into roll-ons**? What equipment do they use for 6 ml and 12 ml, and do they ball-press the rollers?
5. What is the **minimum run** for 6 ml and 12 ml roll-ons? (Our plan is ~500/SKU for the 3 heroes and 100–200 for oil-library SKUs.) Can they do 3 ml vials too? Can they repack bulk oil into 500 g / 1 kg aluminium bottles?
6. Do they source the bottles, rollers and caps, or do we supply them? Who labels, and is batch/expiry coding done in-house?
7. **Line separation:** are the talc and aerosol areas separate from liquid filling? How do they clean between scents?
8. **QC:** do they check incoming oil, keep retained samples per batch, and keep batch manufacturing records? How many years are records kept?
9. Will they sign an **NDA** given their own Z fragrance range? Will they agree in writing not to reuse or replicate our oils?
10. **Pricing:** itemised per-unit costs (filling, labour, components, labelling, cartons). Lead time per run. Can they hold a 3–5-SKU buffer stock, and what does storage cost?
11. **Phase 2 (low priority):** are the Z EDPs and body sprays made here? Do they hold perfumery-alcohol permits?

## Red flags
- No public evidence that they do contract filling. The business may be built only around its own Z brand.
- They sell competing fragrances under their own brand.
- Talc on site means a dust/cross-contamination risk if lines aren't separated.
- **Not yet checked:** any hint that the fill would be subcontracted to a different unit. A loan licence must sit on the licensed premises.
