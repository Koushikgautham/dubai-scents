- **Type / location:** Brand with a claimed own factory in Ajman (type unconfirmed); factory outlet at Jurf Industrial 2, Ajman; office in Deira, Dubai

## What we know
- Beautyworld ME 2022 listing (self-description): founded 2000 by Ali Husain. Makes concentrated perfume oils, bakhoor, water perfumes, body sprays and more, "all made in our factory". The listed address is the Deira office/shop (Deira Palace Hotel Bldg, Shop 3), +971 4 235 1953. Product groups include Compounds and Fragrance Oil.
- Company FAQ: "Naseem Perfumes are completely alcohol-free". Its water-based perfumes are a signature line. This fits our alcohol-free positioning.
- About page lists a "Factory Outlet, Jurf Industrial 2, Ajman, +971 6 569 9517". The page does not say this is the production site. Third-party search snippets (Mixcloud profile) say the factory is in Ajman.
- No private-label/OEM offer found. A "Customize" link on the site is a consumer custom-bottle product.
