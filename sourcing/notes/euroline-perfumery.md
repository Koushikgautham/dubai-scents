# Euroline Perfumery FZC
- **Type / location:** Private-label perfumery (manufacturer claim unconfirmed), Q4-071 SAIF Zone, Sharjah
- **Sources:** https://www.eurolineperfumery.com/private-labels/ ; https://www.eurolineperfumery.com/ (checked 2026-10-20)

## What we know (all from the company's own website)
- Private-label service covering manufacturing, packaging (bottles, caps, custom printing), a "perfume lab" for regulatory and QC support, and design/marketing.
- Site says "all the manufacturing is conducted at our own site" and claims ISO 22716:2007 GMP compliance. Certificate images are shown but not named.
- Products: fragrance oils, perfume sprays, hair/body mists; "oriental and/or French fragrances". Alcohol-free status of the oils is not stated.
- Contacts: +971 55 286 2202 (also WhatsApp), info@eurolineperfumery.com. Retail shops in Bur Dubai (Al Sharafi Centre) and Sharjah (Hilton, Corniche).
- No designer-copy names seen on the homepage (products shown only as "Blends" / "Exclusives").

## Open questions (ask on visit)
- Is the SAIF Zone unit (Q4-071) where compounding and filling happen? May we see it?
- Trade licence activity: manufacturing or trading?
- ISO 22716: certifying body and validity.
- Alcohol-free oils for neat skin use: MOQ, samples, SDS/IFRA/COA, flash points.
- Authorisation letter, exclusivity + escrow, India experience.

## Red flags
- The only source is its own website; no independent listing found yet. Retail shops plus a free-zone unit fits either a manufacturer or a trader with a filling line.
