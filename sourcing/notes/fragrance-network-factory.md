# Fragrance Network Factory
- **Type / location:** Private-label fragrance maker (type not yet confirmed), Ras Al Khor Industrial 3, Dubai
- **Sources:** https://beautyworld-middle-east.ae.messefrankfurt.com/dubai/en/exhibitor-search.detail.html/fragrance-network-factory.html ; trade-fair brochure https://exhibitorsearch.messefrankfurt.com/images/original/document_downloads/10000053202501/435846/1753190413713_835170384.pdf (checked 2026-10-20, via search-result summaries; the listing pages now redirect to the show homepage and the brochure PDF is image-only)

## What we know
- Beautyworld Middle East listings (2024 and 2025) say: established 2018, with private labelling, custom packaging, third-party manufacturing, filling services, and wholesale & distribution. Products include perfumes, oud, oils, diffusers and air care.
- Address per listing: Warehouse No. 1, Toufaik A2, Ras Al Khor Industrial 3, Dubai, +971 4 344 2448. Another listing shows a Nad Al Hamar address, so confirm which is the production site.
- Self-claimed certifications: ISO 9001:2015, ISO 22716:2007 (cosmetics GMP), Emirates Quality Mark. No certifier listing checked yet.
- Own brands: Serenity Perfumes, Aromatic Scents Lab, Flower Scents, Rimal Collection, Liwan.
- Website possibly fragrancenetwork.ae. That domain comes from a third-party listing and is unconfirmed.

## Open questions (ask on visit)
- Do you compound the oils yourselves, or buy compounds and fill? Who is the "manufacturer" on SDS/COA?
- Alcohol-free perfume oils: MOQ per oil, sample policy, flash points.
- ISO 22716 certificate: issuing body and validity.
- Authorisation letter, IFRA (neat Cat 4), exclusivity + escrow, India export experience.

## Red flags
- Address inconsistency between listings. The certifications are self-claims.
