## Gaps and what to verify next
1. **Trade licences.** Ask each lead for a licence copy showing the activity is manufacturing, not trading. This matters most for Black Oud, Euroline and Nabaa Al Danah.
2. **IFRA for neat use.** We sell oil neat in roll-ons, so the IFRA certificate must allow ~100% in Category 4. This is the key question for CPL, because compounds are often certified only for spray dilution.
3. **The five must-haves.** Before December, get written yes/no answers by email on: private label, minimum per oil (we start ~15–20 kg in total), SDS/IFRA/COA, flash point above 60 °C, and the CDSCO authorisation letter. Then spend visit days only on those who say yes.
4. **More leads if needed.** The exhibitor list from the Emirates Perfumes & Oud Exhibition at Expo Centre Sharjah (it ran in October) is the next place to look for small Sharjah/Ajman oil makers.
