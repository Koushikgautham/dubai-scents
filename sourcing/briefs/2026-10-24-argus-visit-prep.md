## Questions to ask (most important first)
**Deal-breakers. Get these answered before you leave:**
1. **Can we see your licence?** Does it cover **category 41, non-hydroalcoholic perfumes**? If not, would you apply to add it, and how long would that take?
2. Will you **host our loan licence** (COS-6 → COS-9)? Have you done one for another brand? What do you need from us?
3. Do you take **third-party / private-label** work at all?
4. **What's the minimum run** for 6 ml and 12 ml roll-ons? We're planning about **500 per scent** for 3 hero SKUs, and **100–200** for small "oil library" runs. Can you also do 3 ml vials?
5. Will **all filling happen at this licensed unit**? A loan licence can't be filled at a different site.

**How they'd do it:**

6. Have you filled **neat, alcohol-free oils into roll-ons** before? With what equipment? Do you press the roller balls on, and how do you check for leaks?
7. How do you clean the line **between scents**? Oil residue is harder to clean off than alcohol.
8. Are the **talc and aerosol areas separate** from liquid filling?
9. Do you source the bottles, rollers and caps, or do we supply them? Do you do labelling, **batch and expiry coding**, and cartons?
10. Can you **repack bulk oil into 500 g / 1 kg aluminium bottles** for our B2B line?
11. **QC:** do you check incoming oil? Do you keep retained samples per batch and batch records? For how long?

**Money and terms:**

12. Can you give us an **itemised per-unit price**: filling, labour, components, labels and cartons? I'll compare it with Clarion's.
13. What's the lead time per run? Can you **store a 3–5-SKU buffer stock** for us, and what does that cost?
14. Will you sign an **NDA** and agree in writing not to reuse or copy our oils, given your own Z fragrances?
15. *(Low priority, for later sprays):* are your EDPs and body sprays made here? Do you hold perfumery-alcohol permits?

Be upfront about timing. Your first real run is likely **around mid-2027**, after the CDSCO import registration and loan licence come through. This visit is to choose your filler.

## What to look at on the floor
| Look at | Good sign | Red flag |
|---|---|---|
| **Licence in hand** | Copy shown; C-1185, Maraimalai Nagar address, categories include perfumes/cat 41, valid to 2028 | "It's with the owner", or it only covers talc/deo |
| **Liquid filling area** | Separate from talc powder; clean, labelled lines | Talc dust in the air near where oil would be filled |
| **Small-bottle filling** | Semi-automatic filler for small bottles, a roller/cap pressing tool, written cleaning steps between products | Everything hand-filled with no records; oil sharing a line with aerosols with no separation |
| **Oil / raw-material storage** | Cool, dark, labelled drums with batch numbers | Unlabelled drums, heat, sunlight |
| **QC corner** | Retained samples by batch, a balance, batch records you can actually see | "We check by smell" |
| **Coding and labelling** | Batch/expiry coder, label applicator, finished Z packs showing proper Rule 34 details | No coding capability |
| **Safety** | Fire extinguishers, ventilation (fragrance alcohol and oils are flammable) | None visible |
| **People** | A named technical or QC person who answers technical questions | Only sales staff; technical questions get deflected |
| **Payments** | Quotes and invoices on letterhead to a company account | Cash only, or a big advance before a trial run |

**Bring:**
- A printed copy of these questions and a notebook.
- A few **empty 6 ml and 12 ml glass roll-on bottles**, so they can see the exact component.
- A small bottle of an alcohol-free oil bought legally in India, to ask for a **test fill and leak check**.

Ask before you take photos. Also confirm the plant is **running on Saturday**, so you see a live line and not an empty floor.
